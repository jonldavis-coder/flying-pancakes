Glossary

Appam
A bowl-shaped fermented rice pancake from Kerala, cooked in a rounded pan. Sri Lankan hoppers are the close cousin.
Arepa
A thick corn cake of Venezuela and Colombia, usually made from precooked corn flour and cooked on a griddle.
Baghrir
A Moroccan semolina pancake cooked on one side so the top fills with holes.
Bannock
A round griddle bread. In Scotland it is often oat. In Canada the wheat baking-powder bread is a later, related food.
Blini
Yeasted pancakes of Russia and its neighbors, especially the food of Maslenitsa.
Boxty
An Irish potato bread. This journal’s recipe is the pan cake of raw and cooked potato.
Cachapa
A Venezuelan pancake of fresh sweet corn.
Casabe
A dry Caribbean cassava flatbread with Taíno roots. The recipe here uses commercial flour.
Chandeleur
February 2, Candlemas, the French day most associated with crêpes. It is not Shrove Tuesday.
Cheela
A North Indian savory pancake, often of chickpea flour, cooked without a long ferment.
Comal
A flat griddle used for tortillas and many other cakes in Mexico.
Crêpe
A thin egg pancake. In France the sweet ones are usually wheat.
Dosa
A South Indian fermented crepe of rice and urad dal.
Dutch baby
An American name for a German oven pancake. The Dutch part is generally a muddle of Deutsch, not a recipe from the Netherlands.
Flapjack
In the United States, a pancake. In Britain, an oat traybake, which is a different food and not in this recipe list.
Galette
In Brittany, usually a buckwheat crêpe. Elsewhere in France the word can mean a different cake.
Griddle
A flat, hot plate. A Scottish girdle is the same idea.
Hoe cake
A Southern cornmeal griddle cake. The hoe-blade story is traditional, not a document this guide can verify.
Hoppers
Sri Lankan bowl-shaped rice pancakes, kin to Kerala appam.
Injera
The sour, spongy teff flatbread of Ethiopia and Eritrea, cooked on one side.
Johnnycake
A cornmeal griddle cake of southern New England. The word’s origin is disputed. Caribbean johnny cakes are related names with their own methods.
Latke
An Ashkenazi potato pancake associated with Hanukkah.
Maslenitsa
The Orthodox week before Great Lent, when blini are the festival food.
Okonomiyaki
A savory Japanese pancake. Osaka mixes the batter. Hiroshima layers it.
Pajeon
A Korean scallion pancake.
Pikelet
In Yorkshire, a crumpet-like cake. In the Midlands, Australia, and New Zealand, a small thick drop scone. This journal keeps them apart.
Pupusa
A stuffed corn cake from El Salvador, cooked on a comal.
Qatayef
Ramadan pancakes of the Levant and Egypt, cooked on one side and filled.
Shrove Tuesday
The day before Ash Wednesday. In Britain and Ireland it is Pancake Day.
Tawa
A flat or slightly curved griddle used for dosa, roti, and many other breads.
Uttapam
A thick, savory pancake of dosa batter with vegetables pressed into the top.
