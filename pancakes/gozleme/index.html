Pronounced GEUZ-leh-meh

A thin griddled flatbread stuffed with spinach and cheese.

30 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 gözleme

History

Gözleme is a Turkish stuffed flatbread cooked on a griddle, a market and home food. Fillings include spinach and white cheese, potato, or minced meat. This recipe is the spinach and cheese one. The dough is unleavened and rolled thin. A sac, a convex griddle, is traditional. A skillet works.

Cultural background

It is folded like a packet and cooked until spotted. Street vendors roll it to order. Lemon on the side is common.

Why it tastes this way

A thin dough cooks fast and tastes of the filling: salty white cheese and wilted spinach. The griddle browns the face without an oven. Spinach and cheese are a pasture and garden combination. The bread is a vehicle. Market cooking is why gözleme is associated with a woman at a sac, a real scene and not a costume.

How it is made

Unleavened dough is filled with spinach and white cheese, folded, and cooked on a griddle.

Authentic touch

Roll the dough thin enough to see a shadow of the filling. Seal the edges with a little water. Cook on medium so the spinach heats and the dough does not scorch.

How it is used

Hot, with lemon.

Field notes

  • Gözleme is defined by the thin dough and a filling, cooked on a griddle.
  • Spinach and white cheese are a classic meatless filling.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 cups chopped spinach
  • 1 cup crumbled white cheese
  • Black pepper
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Mix a soft dough and rest 15 minutes.
  2. Salt the spinach, squeeze it dry, and mix with cheese and pepper.
  3. Divide the dough into 4, roll thin, fill, and fold into packets.
  4. Cook on a medium oiled griddle until both sides are spotted and the filling is hot.
  5. Serve with lemon.
  6. Do not overfill.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A potato filling is another meatless gözleme.
  • Minced meat is a common filling and adds meat.

Pairings

  • lemon
  • tea
