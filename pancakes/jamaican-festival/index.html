Arriving in Montego Bay

Jamaican festival

You come in by Boat. Jamaica · Caribbean.

Pronounced FES-tih-vuhl

Sweet cornmeal and wheat dumplings, fried until deep gold, with a soft center.

20 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 6 · 12 dumplings

History

Festival is a sweet fried dumpling of cornmeal and wheat flour, sold beside fried fish at Jamaican stalls. It is related to johnny cakes. A johnny cake is usually less sweet and may be baked or cooked in a skillet rather than fried. Festival is the fried, sweeter one. The fish is the usual partner and is not part of this dough. Cornmeal here is a later street bread, not a claim about an ancient corn recipe.

Cultural background

Beach and market cooks shape the dough into short logs and fry them in the same oil they use for fish, though the dough itself is meatless. The name festival is the one on the stall sign. People still argue the johnny-cake family tree. The frying and the sugar are the part you can taste.

Why it tastes this way

Sugar in the dough browns in the oil and makes the crust taste of caramel as well as of corn. Cornmeal keeps the center a little grainy. Wheat flour gives enough gluten to hold a log together. Butter adds a dairy note. Because they are fried, the outside is crisp while the inside stays dumpling-soft. They are not spicy.

How it is made

Mix wheat flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, salt, butter, and water into a soft dough. Shape logs and fry them in oil until deep gold and cooked through.

Authentic touch

The dough should be soft and slightly sticky. Shape it with oiled hands into short, thick sticks, not thin strings. Fry until the color is a deep gold. A pale festival is still raw in the middle.

How it is used

Eat them hot next to fried fish, or on their own as a sweet street snack. This recipe is only the dumpling.

Field notes

  • Festival is fried. That is the difference people taste next to a baked johnny cake.
  • The dough is cornmeal plus wheat, so it is not gluten-free.
  • The usual plate includes fried fish. The fish is not in this recipe.

Ingredients

  • 120 g wheat flour
  • 120 g fine cornmeal
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 30 g butter
  • 80 ml water
  • Neutral oil for frying

Steps

  1. Stir the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Rub in the butter.
  2. Add the water and mix to a soft dough. Rest it for 10 minutes.
  3. With oiled hands, shape 12 short logs.
  4. Fry in oil over medium heat, turning, until deep gold and cooked through, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Drain and serve hot.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A pinch of vanilla or a little grated nutmeg is a home addition.
  • Leave out the butter and use oil in the dough if you want the cakes dairy-free.
  • Bake them and you have moved toward a johnny cake. Festival, as written here, is fried.

Pairings

  • Fried fish and festival, the stall plate, which adds fish
  • A dab of butter on a plain one
  • Pepper sauce for contrast, not in the dough
