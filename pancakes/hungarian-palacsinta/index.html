Pronounced PAW-law-chin-taw

Thin pancakes rolled with jam, walnut, or sweet cheese.

20 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 crepes

History

Palacsinta are Hungary’s crepes, filled with apricot jam, ground walnuts and sugar, cocoa, or túró cheese. They are a national dessert. Gundel palacsinta, with a walnut and rum filling and a chocolate sauce, is a restaurant elaboration. This page is the home cake with jam or walnuts. Walnuts are a tree nut, so the written filling here is apricot jam, and walnuts are a named variation.

Cultural background

A stack of filled palacsinta is Sunday lunch dessert. The pancake is thin and only lightly sweet. The filling does the work.

Why it tastes this way

Apricot jam is a Hungarian pantry flavor, tart and fragrant, and it suits a mild crepe. Walnuts, when used, are a local tree crop ground with sugar, a filling that tastes of oil and bitterness under the sweet. The crepe itself is egg and milk, a Habsburg-era central European form shared with palatschinken and palačinke, each with its own fillings.

How it is made

Thin egg pancakes are cooked and rolled with apricot jam.

Authentic touch

Spread the jam thinly and roll while warm. A thick layer of cold jam splits the crepe.

How it is used

Warm, dusted with sugar if you like.

Field notes

  • Jam, walnut, cocoa, and túró are the fillings to know. This recipe writes the jam so the base cake is nut-free.
  • Gundel palacsinta is a richer restaurant dish, not the only palacsinta.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup sparkling water
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1/2 cup apricot jam

Steps

  1. Whisk the batter, including the sparkling water, and rest 15 minutes.
  2. Cook thin crepes in an oiled pan.
  3. Spread each with a little jam and roll.
  4. Serve warm.
  5. The water makes the batter lighter.
  6. Do not boil the jam or it will scorch.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Ground walnuts and sugar are a classic filling and add tree nuts.
  • Túró, sweet farmer cheese, is the cheese filling.

Pairings

  • sugar
