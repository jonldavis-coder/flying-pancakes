History

Matzo brei is broken matzo soaked, mixed with egg, and fried. It is Passover food, when leavened bread is out of the Ashkenazi kitchen, and it is also a year-round breakfast for people who simply like it. The name is Yiddish: matzo cooked with egg. American Jewish households, especially in the Northeast, treat the salty-versus-sweet argument as part of the dish.

Cultural background

Some cooks fry it as a pancake-like cake. Others scramble it. Both are matzo brei. This recipe makes small cakes, so it sits honestly among pancakes, with the scrambled way noted as a variation.

Why it tastes this way

Matzo is an unleavened cracker of flour and water, baked fast, which is why it tastes toasty and dry. A short soak gives it just enough water to turn custardy with egg. At Passover the method exists because leavening is set aside. Salt and pepper make it breakfast. Sugar or jam makes it the other family. The taste is cracker and egg, not batter and baking powder.

How it is made

Sheets of matzo are wetted, mixed with beaten egg, and fried in cakes in a lightly oiled pan.

Authentic touch

Soak the matzo until it bends, not until it is porridge. Thirty seconds in water is often enough for thin matzo. Squeeze gently.

How it is used

Salt and pepper, or sugar and jam. Sour cream if you eat dairy with it.

Field notes

  • Salty and sweet matzo brei are both traditional in different families.
  • The cake shape is one method. Scrambled matzo brei is the other.

Ingredients

  • 2 sheets matzo
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil or chicken fat

Steps

  1. Break the matzo, rinse it under water until flexible, and squeeze out the extra water.
  2. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper and stir in the matzo.
  3. Heat oil in a skillet.
  4. Fry as two or four small cakes, turning once, until the egg is set and the edges brown.
  5. Serve salty, or with sugar and jam.
  6. Eat it at once. It toughens as it sits.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Scramble it in the pan instead of shaping cakes if that is your house style.
  • A grated onion in the egg is a savory turn.

Pairings

  • salt
  • jam
  • sour cream
  • applesauce
