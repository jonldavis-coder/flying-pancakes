Salt-cod and onion fritters, crisp outside and soft inside.

History

Pataniscas are Portuguese salt-cod fritters, a batter of soaked bacalhau, onion, parsley, egg, and flour, fried in spoonfuls. Salt cod has been a Portuguese staple for centuries, tied to the Atlantic fishery. The fritter is tavern and home food. It is a fried cake, included here as a savory cousin, and it is not a sweet pancake.

Cultural background

They are eaten hot, with rice or salad, or as a snack. The cod must be soaked or it will be too salty to eat. That soak is part of the recipe.

Why it tastes this way

Salt cod tastes of the sea and of the salt that preserved it for ships and for inland cupboards before refrigeration. Soaking pulls out enough salt to make it food again. Onion and parsley freshen the batter. The fry gives a crust. Portugal’s long cod trade is the reason a fish fritter, not a corn cake, is the national snack of this kind.

How it is made

Soaked salt cod is mixed with onion, parsley, egg, and flour and fried as small cakes.

Authentic touch

Soak the cod in cold water in the refrigerator, changing the water, until a boiled flake tastes seasoned rather than punishing. Then shred it. Do not skip the soak.

How it is used

Hot, with lemon or with rice and salad.

Field notes

  • Salt cod has to be soaked. That is not optional color.
  • Pataniscas are fritters. They sit in this journal as a savory fried cake.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces salt cod, soaked and flaked
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • Black pepper
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Soak the cod in the refrigerator, changing the water, until it is palatably salty, then simmer it 5 minutes and flake it. Discard bones and skin.
  2. Mix cod, onion, parsley, eggs, flour, and pepper into a thick batter.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in 1/2 inch of oil until golden.
  4. Drain.
  5. Serve hot with lemon.
  6. Taste a flake before you mix. If it is still too salty, soak longer.

Fry over steady heat. Soak salt cod in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A spoon of beer in the batter makes it lighter.
  • Fresh cod is a different, less salty fritter and is not bacalhau.

Pairings

  • lemon
  • rice
  • salad
