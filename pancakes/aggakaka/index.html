Pronounced EG-ah-koh-kah

A thick, fried egg cake from Skåne, served with bacon and lingonberry.

10 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · one cake

History

Äggakaka is a Scanian egg cake, associated with Skåne in southern Sweden. It is thicker and more egg than a usual pancake, fried in a pan, and served with pork and lingonberries. The contrast of salty meat, rich egg, and tart berries is the regional plate.

Cultural background

It is a meal, not a stack with syrup. The cake can be cut into wedges. Some cooks make it almost like a thick omelette.

Why it tastes this way

Eggs are the body of the cake, so it tastes custardy and browned rather than floury. Skåne is farming country, and the plate uses what a farm had: eggs, pork, and preserved lingonberries. The berries cut the fat. Flour is only there to keep the egg from scrambling into crumbs.

How it is made

Beaten eggs, a little flour, and milk are fried as one thick cake, then cut.

Authentic touch

Cook it slower than a crepe so the center sets without the bottom turning black. Cover the pan. A wet center is undercooked egg.

How it is used

With fried pork or bacon and lingonberry jam.

Field notes

  • Äggakaka is a Skåne dish. It is not the same as a thin Swedish pancake.
  • Lingonberry and pork are the traditional partners.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 8 ounces bacon or salt pork
  • Lingonberry jam, to serve

Steps

  1. Fry the bacon and set it aside. Leave a little fat in the pan.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, flour, and salt.
  3. Pour into the pan and cook covered over medium-low until the center is just set.
  4. Brown the top under a broiler or flip carefully.
  5. Cut into wedges.
  6. Serve with the bacon and lingonberry jam.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A meatless version leaves off the pork and is then a different plate. The classic includes it.
  • More flour makes it more like an ordinary pancake and less like äggakaka.

Pairings

  • bacon
  • lingonberry jam
