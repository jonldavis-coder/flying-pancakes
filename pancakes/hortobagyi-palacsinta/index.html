Arriving in Hortobágy

Hortobágyi palacsinta

You come in by Train. Hungary · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced HOR-toh-bah-dyi PAH-lah-chin-tah

Hungarian crepes filled with paprika chicken, folded, and baked with sour cream.

30 min prep · 45 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 filled crepes

History

Hortobágyi palacsinta are crepes filled with a paprika meat stew, folded, and served with a paprika sauce and sour cream. The name points to the Hortobágy plain. Stories that credit a single inventor or a single fair are disputed, so this page does not name one. It should not be described as an ancient herdsman's recipe. It is a restaurant and home dish of filled paprika pancakes. The sweet jam palacsinta is the other page. Sweet paprika makes the heat mild, not a chile blast.

Cultural background

Paprika, onion, and meat are the core of the filling. Veal is traditional in many restaurant versions. This recipe uses chicken thigh, which is a common home substitute, and says so. The crepes are wheat, egg, and milk.

Why it tastes this way

Onion cooked in fat and sweet paprika tastes sweet and earthy, not burnt, if the paprika never fries dry. Chicken takes up that sauce. The crepe is mild. Sour cream cools the paprika and turns the sauce pink. The heat stays mild because this recipe uses sweet paprika, not hot.

How it is made

Chicken is simmered with onion and sweet paprika, chopped, and folded into thin crepes. A sauce of the cooking liquid and sour cream is poured over, and the dish is warmed.

Authentic touch

Take the pan off the heat before you stir in the paprika, so it does not scorch. Fold the crepes rather than rolling them tight. Sour cream goes on as a sauce. Do not tell the Hortobágy story as a settled invention.

How it is used

Hot, as a savory main course. It is a meat dish.

Field notes

  • The name points to the Hortobágy. A single inventor story is disputed.
  • It is a filled paprika crepe, not the sweet jam palacsinta.
  • This recipe uses chicken and says so. Many restaurant versions use veal.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain wheat flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 300 ml whole milk
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 40 g unsalted butter or lard, divided
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 500 g boneless chicken thigh, cut into small pieces
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 200 ml sour cream

Steps

  1. Make a thin crepe batter with the flour, eggs, milk, and salt. Cook eight crepes in a little of the fat and set them aside.
  2. Cook the onion in the remaining fat until soft. Add the chicken and a pinch of salt and cook until it loses its raw color. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the paprika, then add 150 ml water and simmer until the chicken is tender and the sauce is thick.
  3. Chop the chicken. Mix a few spoons of sour cream into the sauce. Fill and fold the crepes around the meat.
  4. Lay them in a dish, spoon over the remaining sauce and sour cream, and warm at 180°C for 10 minutes.

Cook the chicken until it is no longer pink. Scorched paprika tastes bitter, so do not fry the spice in a dry pan.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Veal instead of chicken
  • Hot paprika, which raises the heat above mild and should be labeled if you use it

Pairings

  • The paprika sour-cream sauce
  • A cucumber salad
  • Plain rice, if you want a starch beside the crepe
