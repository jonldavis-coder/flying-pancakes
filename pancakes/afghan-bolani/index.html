Arriving in Kabul

Afghan bolani

You come in by Plane. Afghanistan · Central Asia.

Pronounced bo-LAH-nee

Crisp stuffed flatbread filled with seasoned potato and leek, mild with green chile.

40 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 large stuffed flatbreads

History

Bolani is an Afghan stuffed flatbread. A thin wheat dough is filled with potato, leek, or pumpkin, folded, and cooked on a griddle with a slick of oil until the outside blisters. In Kabul it is street food and home food, cut into wedges and eaten with a green chutney. This filling uses potato and leek together, with a mild green chile. It is not a batter pancake. Central Asia is the region this journal uses for Afghanistan.

Cultural background

Bolani is made for guests and for ordinary afternoons. The filling should be dry enough not to tear the dough. Cilantro and chile are mixed into the potato, not served only as a sauce. A yogurt dip is common and is optional dairy beside a vegan bread.

Why it tastes this way

The crust tastes of toasted wheat and a little frying oil. Potato makes the inside soft and mild, and leek adds a sweet onion flavor without the sharpness of raw scallion. A chopped green chile is warm rather than burning, which is why the heat is mild. Salt and cilantro keep the filling from eating like plain mashed potato.

How it is made

Boil and mash potato, then mix it with softened leek, cilantro, green chile, and salt. Roll dough into thin rounds, fill half, seal, and cook on an oiled griddle until both sides are golden and crisp. Serve with a cilantro chutney.

Authentic touch

Drain the potato filling well and seal it inside a thin dough. Bolani is a stuffed flatbread. A wet filling splits the crust. The chile in this batch is mild.

How it is used

Bolani is a snack, a street food, and a savory share at the table. It is cut and eaten by hand. Yogurt on the side is optional and adds dairy. A meat filling is not this recipe.

Field notes

  • Bolani is a stuffed flatbread, not a batter pancake.
  • Potato and leek are both in this filling. Pumpkin is another common choice.
  • A green chile makes the heat mild rather than hot.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour (about 250 g)
  • 3/4 teaspoon fine salt, divided
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 leek, finely chopped and softened in a dry pan
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 mild green chile, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons oil for the filling and the pan

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, half the salt, and the water into a soft dough. Knead and rest it for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the mashed potato, softened leek, cilantro, chile, remaining salt, and a spoon of oil. The chile is mild. The filling should be thick, not wet.
  3. Divide the dough into 4 and roll each piece thin. Spread filling on half and fold. Seal the edges. This is a stuffed flatbread.
  4. Cook on a griddle filmed with oil, turning once, until both sides are golden and the dough is cooked through.
  5. Cut into wedges. Serve with chutney. Yogurt is optional and is not in the dough.

The dough contains wheat. The chile is mild but still a chile. Oil on the griddle is hot.

Refrigerate leftover bolani and reheat on a skillet so the crust crisps. Do not leave the potato filling out in a warm room. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Leek alone, the green called gandana in many Afghan kitchens, is a classic filling.
  • Pumpkin replaces potato in season and stays vegan if you skip the yogurt.
  • A hotter chile would change the mild tag. This one stays mild.

Pairings

  • Cilantro chutney
  • Plain yogurt, which adds dairy
  • Black tea
