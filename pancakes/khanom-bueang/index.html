Pronounced kah-nom boo-ang

Crisp Thai crepes folded over a sweet meringue-like filling and savory threads.

20 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · about 8 crepes

History

Khanom bueang are crisp Thai crepes, sometimes called Thai tacos, filled with a sweet egg-white cream and, classically, a savory orange shred of egg yolk cooked with sugar, plus sometimes dried shrimp. The full classic is elaborate. This home version makes the crisp crepe and a simple sweet filling of whipped egg white and sugar, and it says the shrimp and foi thong are the fuller street version.

Cultural background

The crepe must be thin and crisp. The filling is sweet. They are a snack, not a wrap for dinner.

Why it tastes this way

Rice flour in a thin batter fries crisp, unlike a soft French crepe. The sweet filling is egg white and sugar, a confection. Street versions add a salty shrimp note, the sweet-salt habit of many Thai snacks. This written filling skips the shrimp so the allergens stay wheat and egg. The crisp shell is still the point.

How it is made

A thin rice-and-wheat batter is cooked crisp and filled with a sweet meringue.

Authentic touch

Spread the batter paper-thin and let it crisp before you lift it. Fill and fold immediately. It softens if it waits.

How it is used

Immediately.

Field notes

  • Khanom bueang should be crisp. A soft crepe is a different snack.
  • This filling is the sweet one. Shrimp is the variation.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup rice flour
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 egg white, plus 1 egg white for the filling
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Whisk the flours, water, and 1 egg white into a thin batter.
  2. Cook paper-thin crepes until crisp.
  3. Whip the second white with the sugar to a soft foam.
  4. Fill and fold at once.
  5. Serve immediately.
  6. A soft crepe means it needed more heat or less batter.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Dried shrimp on the filling is the savory street note and adds shellfish.
  • Foi thong, sweet egg threads, is the classic extra and is more work.

Pairings

  • whipped egg white
