Arriving in Denizli

Turkish gözleme

You come in by Train. Turkey · Middle East.

Pronounced gurz-leh-meh

Thin stuffed flatbread, griddled, with spinach and salty white cheese in the fold.

40 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 large flatbreads

History

Gözleme is a stuffed flatbread. A soft wheat dough is rolled thin, filled, sealed, and cooked on a sac, the wide convex griddle, or in a heavy pan. It is a village and street food across Turkey, not a batter you pour. Spinach and white cheese is a common filling. Potato and minced meat are others. This recipe is the spinach and cheese one, cooked on a griddle, from a Denizli-style home kitchen.

Cultural background

At markets, the cook rolls the dough in the open and fills it to order. At home it is a way to turn greens and cheese into a meal. The bread is cut into pieces as soon as it leaves the pan. It is savory breakfast, lunch, or a snack.

Why it tastes this way

The dough tastes of wheat and salt, with brown spots from the griddle. Spinach keeps a green, slightly metallic flavor if it is only wilted, not boiled to mush. Salty white cheese seasons the filling so the bread does not need a sauce. A slick of butter on the finished bread tastes milky and rich. There is no sugar.

How it is made

Mix a soft dough and rest it. Wilt spinach with onion, salt it, and mix it with crumbled white cheese. Roll each dough piece thin, spread filling on half, fold, and cook on a hot sac or skillet, turning, until blistered. Brush with a little butter.

Authentic touch

Roll the dough thin, seal the filling inside, and cook it on a griddle. Say what it is: a stuffed flatbread, not a pancake. A minced-meat filling is a different batch and is not vegetarian.

How it is used

Gözleme is a savory meal or a street snack, often breakfast. It is eaten with tea and sometimes with yogurt on the side. It is not a dessert. The meat-filled version is common and is not the diet listed here.

Field notes

  • Gözleme is a stuffed flatbread cooked on a griddle, not a poured pancake.
  • Spinach and white cheese is one of the usual fillings.
  • A sac is the wide metal griddle many cooks still use for it.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups wheat flour (about 310 g)
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt, divided
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 300 g spinach, chopped
  • 150 g salty white cheese, crumbled
  • 1 tablespoon butter, for finishing

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, half the salt, water, yogurt, and olive oil into a soft dough. Knead until smooth and rest it for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the onion in a dry pan until soft, add the spinach and the rest of the salt, and cook until the greens collapse and the pan is almost dry. Cool, then mix in the cheese.
  3. Divide the dough into 4. Roll each piece very thin. This is a stuffed flatbread, not a batter.
  4. Spread filling on half of each round, fold the other half over, and press the edges.
  5. Cook on a hot griddle or sac, turning once, until both sides are spotted and the dough is cooked. Brush with butter and cut into pieces.

The dough contains wheat. Yogurt, cheese, and butter are dairy. A meat filling is not part of this version.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry pan. Do not leave the cheese filling at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A potato and onion filling can be made without cheese if you also change the dough fat. This written filling contains dairy.
  • Minced meat with pepper is a widespread filling and is not vegetarian.
  • Cooking on a flat pan instead of a sac still counts if the dough is thin and turned.

Pairings

  • Black tea
  • Plain yogurt
  • A wedge of lemon
