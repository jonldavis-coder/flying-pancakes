Pronounced m-beh-ZHOO

A crisp cassava-starch and cheese cake, rich and salty.

15 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 6 · one 9-inch cake

History

Mbeju, or mbeyú, is a Paraguayan cake of cassava starch and cheese, cooked on a griddle or in a pan until the outside is crisp. It is a traditional breakfast and snack, part of a cuisine that also includes chipa and sopa paraguaya. Cheese and starch are the body. An egg helps it hold.

Cultural background

It should be thin enough to crisp and rich enough to taste of cheese. It is eaten with coffee or cocido. Fresh farmer cheese or a dry queso is better than a wet mozzarella.

Why it tastes this way

Cassava starch crisps, and a salty cheese melts into it, which is the Paraguayan combination of a local starch and a dairy farm. The flavor is salty, toasted, and rich, not sweet. An egg binds. The griddle is the tool. It is a national breakfast cake, not a sweet pancake.

How it is made

Cassava starch, cheese, egg, and milk or cream are cooked as a flat cake in a skillet.

Authentic touch

Grate the cheese dry. Pat the dough thin in a buttered pan and cook it patiently until the bottom is a real crust. Wet cheese makes it steam.

How it is used

Warm, in wedges, with coffee.

Field notes

  • Mbeju is cassava starch and cheese, crisp on the griddle.
  • Use a dry cheese or the cake steams.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cassava starch
  • 1 cup finely grated dry cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Steps

  1. Mix starch, cheese, egg, milk, and salt into a soft dough.
  2. Pat it thin in a buttered skillet.
  3. Cook covered over medium-low until the bottom is a real crust.
  4. Brown the top.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and cut.
  6. The cheese should be dry.

Use commercial cassava flour or yuca sold ready to cook. Do not process bitter cassava at home from these pages.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Anise is sometimes in the dough.
  • Beiju is the plainer starch bread without cheese.

Pairings

  • coffee
