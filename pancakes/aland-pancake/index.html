Pronounced OH-lahnd

A baked rice and cardamom pancake from the Åland Islands.

30 min prep · 45 min cook · Serves 8 · one 8-inch dish

History

Åland pancake is a baked rice porridge pancake from the Åland Islands, which are Swedish-speaking and autonomous within Finland. Cardamom and rice porridge are the base. It is a local dessert and café food, served with jam, often prune jam, and whipped cream. It is distinct from a thin lettu and from Gotland’s saffron pancake, though both are baked rice cakes of the Baltic.

Cultural background

The porridge is cooked, cooled a little, enriched, and baked until the top browns. Prune jam is the usual partner. Another dark jam is a fair substitute if you name it honestly.

Why it tastes this way

Rice cooked in milk tastes sweet and substantial, and cardamom perfumes it the way it perfumes Nordic buns. Åland’s position between Sweden and Finland shows in the language and in this cake, which sits beside both countries’ oven pancakes and keeps its own name. Prune jam is tart and dark, a preserved fruit that stands up to cream.

How it is made

Rice porridge is mixed with egg, sugar, and cardamom and baked in a buttered dish.

Authentic touch

Cook the rice fully before baking, or the finished cake has hard grains. The bake is for setting and browning, not for cooking raw rice.

How it is used

With prune jam or another dark jam, and whipped cream.

Field notes

  • Åland pancake is a named island dish, not a generic baked custard.
  • Prune jam is the traditional fruit partner.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup short-grain rice
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cardamom
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Butter for the dish

Steps

  1. Simmer rice and milk until the rice is soft and the porridge is thick. Cool 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in eggs, sugar, cardamom, flour, and salt.
  3. Pour into a buttered dish.
  4. Bake at 375°F about 40 minutes until set and brown.
  5. Rest 10 minutes.
  6. Serve with prune jam and cream.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Gotland’s pancake uses saffron. This one uses cardamom. Do not treat them as one recipe.
  • A gluten-free version can skip the spoon of flour. The rice is the body.

Pairings

  • prune jam
  • whipped cream
