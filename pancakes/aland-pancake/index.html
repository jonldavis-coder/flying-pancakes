Arriving in Åland

Åland pancake

You come in by Boat. Finland · Northern Europe.

Pronounced OH-land pancake

A baked porridge pancake from Åland, cardamom-scented, with prune jam.

25 min prep · 45 min cook · Serves 8 · 1 baking dish, 8 squares

History

The Åland pancake is a baked pancake made from a cooked porridge, usually semolina or rice, with eggs, milk, sugar, and cardamom. This recipe uses semolina. A rice-porridge version is a real variation. It is the signature sweet of the Åland Islands, which are an autonomous part of Finland, so the country pin is Finland. Prune jam and whipped cream are the serving. It is not a pan-fried Finnish lettu.

Cultural background

It is dessert and celebration food, cut in squares from a baking dish. Cardamom is the spice of the batter. The texture is closer to a firm porridge pudding with a browned top than to a crepe.

Why it tastes this way

Semolina makes it creamy and slightly resilient. Cardamom is floral and piney. Sugar and milk round it. The oven browns the top so there is a crust over a soft interior. Prune jam is dark and tart against the mild cake. Cream carries the cardamom.

How it is made

Semolina is cooked in milk with cardamom into a porridge, enriched with eggs and sugar, baked until set and brown, and served with prune jam.

Authentic touch

Cook the porridge fully before the eggs go in, and cool it a little so the eggs do not scramble. Serve prune jam. Cardamom belongs in the batter.

How it is used

Warm or cold, in squares, with prune jam and whipped cream, as a dessert.

Field notes

  • Åland pancake is baked from porridge. It is not a fried lettu.
  • Semolina and rice versions both exist. This page uses semolina.
  • Prune jam and whipped cream are the usual plate.

Ingredients

  • 1 liter whole milk
  • 100 g semolina
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 20 g unsalted butter, for the dish
  • Prune jam and whipped cream, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat the milk with the cardamom and salt. Rain in the semolina, stirring, and cook until you have a thick porridge. Stir in the sugar. Cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Beat the eggs and stir them into the porridge.
  3. Spread the mixture in a buttered baking dish. Bake at 200°C until set and browned on top, about 40 minutes.
  4. Cut in squares and serve with prune jam and whipped cream.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Rice porridge instead of semolina, which changes the grain
  • Finnish lettu, the thin pan pancake, which is a different page

Pairings

  • Prune jam
  • Whipped cream
  • A little extra ground cardamom
