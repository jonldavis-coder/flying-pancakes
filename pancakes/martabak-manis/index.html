A thick, sweet, yeasted pancake folded over chocolate, cheese, and peanuts.

History

Martabak manis, also terang bulan, is a thick Indonesian sweet pancake sold at night stalls, folded over butter, sugar, chocolate sprinkles, cheese, and peanuts. It is a different food from martabak telur, the savory stuffed one. The yeasted batter is cakey. The fillings are abundant. Peanuts and chocolate are in this recipe, so it is not nut-free.

Cultural background

A wide pan and a lid are the tools. The cake is thick. Vendors cut it into wedges after folding.

Why it tastes this way

Yeast and egg make a soft, tall crumb that can hold a sweet, salty mess of cheese and sugar. Peanuts are the crunch. Chocolate sprinkles are the modern stall habit, not an ancient spice. Cheese against sugar is the Indonesian sweet-salty pairing. Night markets are where it lives, which is why it is a street dessert rather than a family breakfast.

How it is made

A thick yeast batter is cooked covered in a wide pan, topped, and folded.

Authentic touch

Cook covered on low heat until the center is set. Add the fillings, fold, and cut. If the heat is high, the bottom burns and the middle is raw.

How it is used

Warm, in wedges.

Field notes

  • Martabak manis is the thick sweet night-market pancake.
  • Cheese and peanuts on a sweet cake are the usual stall combination.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate sprinkles
  • 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
  • 1/4 cup grated cheese

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, egg, and milk. Rest 1 hour.
  2. Cook the thick batter in a covered wide pan over low heat until set.
  3. Spread butter, chocolate, peanuts, and cheese, and fold.
  4. Cut into wedges.
  5. The center must be cooked before you fill it.
  6. Peanuts are in this topping.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Skip peanuts only if you rename the expectation. They are part of the usual stall topping.
  • Martabak telur is the savory pancake.
