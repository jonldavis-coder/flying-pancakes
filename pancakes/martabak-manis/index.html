Martabak manis

You come in by Plane. Indonesia · Southeast Asia.

Pronounced MAR-tah-bahk MAH-nees

A thick, sweet pancake, also called terang bulan, topped with chocolate, cheese, and peanuts.

20 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 8 · 1 large pancake, 8 squares

History

Martabak manis is Indonesia's thick sweet street pancake, also called terang bulan, the bright moon, because of the round. A yeasted or well-risen wheat batter is cooked in a wide pan, then covered with butter, sugar, chocolate, cheese, or peanuts and folded or cut in squares. This recipe includes peanuts, milk chocolate, and cheese, so the allergens are peanut and dairy as well as wheat and egg. It is a night-market dessert. The savory stuffed martabak telur is a different dish.

Cultural background

Vendors pour one large pancake and sell it by the slice. The crumb is full of holes, closer to a sweet flatbread than to a crepe. Toppings vary by stall and by the buyer's order. Writing chocolate, cheese, and peanuts matches a common plate and the allergen tags.

Why it tastes this way

A thick batter traps air, so the crumb tastes soft and slightly yeasty under a sweet crust. Butter and condensed milk make it rich. Chocolate and cheese are a salty-sweet pair that Indonesian street sweets use often. Peanuts add roast and crunch. The result is heavy, sweet, and meant to be shared. It is not a crisp turnover like Malaysian apam balik, even when both use peanuts.

How it is made

Cook one thick sweet pancake, cover it with butter, chocolate, cheese, and peanuts, and cut it in squares.

Authentic touch

Cook the pancake covered and slowly so the center sets without burning. Add the toppings while the surface is still hot enough to melt chocolate and cheese, then cut.

How it is used

Share it warm, in squares, as a street dessert. It is too rich for a large solo portion.

Field notes

  • Martabak manis is also called terang bulan.
  • Peanuts, chocolate, and cheese are a common topping set. This recipe includes the peanuts.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose wheat flour
  • 1 teaspoon dry yeast
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 40 grams milk chocolate, chopped
  • 40 grams mild cheese, grated
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, crushed

Steps

  1. Stir flour, yeast, sugar, egg, water, and condensed milk into a thick batter. Rest until bubbly, about 45 minutes. The rise is not active prep.
  2. Butter a wide pan over medium-low heat. Pour in the batter and spread it thick.
  3. Cover and cook until the top is set and full of holes and the bottom is brown. Flip if you can do it cleanly, or cook covered longer.
  4. Dot with butter. Scatter chocolate, cheese, and peanuts so they melt and stick.
  5. Cut into squares and serve warm.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Sesame and sugar only, which would drop the peanut and could drop the dairy if you skip cheese
  • A banana under the chocolate

Pairings

  • Hot tea
  • An extra sprinkle of sugar
  • A salty cheese if you want more contrast
