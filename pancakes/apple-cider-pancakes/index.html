Arriving in Hudson

Apple cider pancakes

You come in by Hot air balloon. United States · North America.

Reduced sweet cider and soft apple pieces in a lightly spiced wheat cake.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 10 pancakes

History

Apple cider pancakes are a fall breakfast in New York and New England cider country. Sweet cider is reduced so its flavor is strong enough to taste in a wheat batter, and a little diced apple goes in too. The acid in the cider also helps the soda lift the cakes. Hudson, New York, is the map pin.

Cultural background

Use sweet cider, sometimes sold as fresh apple cider, not bottled vinegar. Reducing it on the stove is what concentrates the flavor. The diced apple is the texture.

Why it tastes this way

Unreduced cider tastes thin once it is mixed with flour. Boiling it down leaves a sharper apple syrup. The diced apple softens on the griddle and stays in pieces. Cinnamon is a background, not the whole flavor.

How it is made

Griddled wheat batter made with reduced sweet cider and diced apple.

Authentic touch

Reduce the cider before it goes into the bowl. Pouring cold cider straight into a pancake mix will not taste like this.

How it is used

Hot, with butter. More cider, warmed, can be poured on lightly.

Field notes

  • Reducing sweet cider is what makes the apple flavor strong enough to taste in batter.
  • The acid in cider reacts with baking soda and helps the cakes rise.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sweet apple cider
  • 1/2 cup small-diced peeled apple
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the griddle

Steps

  1. Boil the cider in a small saucepan until it reduces to 1/2 cup. Cool it.
  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. Stir in the reduced cider, milk, egg, and melted butter. Fold in the diced apple.
  4. Butter a griddle over medium heat. Pour 1/4 cup cakes.
  5. Flip when the underside is brown and the top is set. Cook until the apple pieces are tender.
  6. Serve with butter.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Leave the diced apple out if you only want the cider in the crumb.
  • A pinch more cinnamon is plenty. Nutmeg goes bitter quickly.

Pairings

  • Butter
  • Warm reduced cider
  • Maple syrup
