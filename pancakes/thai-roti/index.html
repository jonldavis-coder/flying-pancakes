Thai roti

You come in by Boat. Thailand · Southeast Asia.

Pronounced ROH-tee

A stretched, griddled wheat bread, crisp and chewy, wrapped around banana and condensed milk.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 stuffed rotis

History

Thai roti is the street bread cooked on a griddle, stretched thin, fried in oil or margarine, and often filled with banana and sweetened condensed milk. Related griddled breads traveled with Indian and Malay trade and with cooks who moved through the region. This page does not name one inventor. In Thailand the banana-and-condensed-milk roti is its own street dessert, different from a curry-dipped roti canai and from a Sinhalese pol roti. The dough is wheat, laminated or stretched until it flakes.

Cultural background

Night markets in Bangkok sell it from a round steel pan. The cook slaps the dough thin, drops it on oil, cracks an egg in some versions, or lays in banana and condensed milk and folds the bread shut. This recipe is the banana fold, without egg, so the allergens are wheat and dairy.

Why it tastes this way

Stretched dough fries into crisp sheets and a chewy center, the texture of a flatbread that has seen a lot of fat. Banana steams inside and turns sweet. Condensed milk is the sauce, thick and milky, not a fruit syrup. A little salt in the dough keeps the sugar from being one note. The flavor is a street dessert built by trade in bread technique and by local fruit and canned milk.

How it is made

Rest a soft wheat dough, stretch it thin, griddle it with banana and condensed milk, and fold it shut.

Authentic touch

Stretch the dough until you can almost see through it, then cook it in enough oil to blister. A thick unstretched round will taste like plain bread, not street roti.

How it is used

Cut the folded roti into pieces and eat it hot, with extra condensed milk if you want. It is a street snack and a dessert.

Field notes

  • Thai banana roti sits in a family of griddled breads linked by Indian and Malay trade.
  • This page does not name a single inventor for that family.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose wheat flour
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil, plus more for the pan
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, water, and 2 tablespoons of oil into a soft dough. Knead until smooth. Rest 30 minutes. The rest is not active prep.
  2. Divide into four. Stretch or roll each piece very thin.
  3. Oil a griddle over medium heat. Lay the dough on it and cook until blistered and spotted.
  4. Flip, arrange banana on half, drizzle condensed milk, and fold the bread over.
  5. Cook until the outside is crisp and the banana is warm. Cut and serve.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • An egg cracked on the griddle bread, which adds an egg allergen
  • Chocolate condensed milk, still dairy

Pairings

  • Sweetened condensed milk
  • A cup of tea
  • A squeeze of lime if you like a sharper bite
