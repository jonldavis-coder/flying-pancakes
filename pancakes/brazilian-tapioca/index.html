History

Tapioca crepes are a Brazilian street and home breakfast made from gummed tapioca starch, not a wheat batter. The starch is moistened until it clumps, then scattered in a pan, where it binds into a crepe. Cheese or coconut and condensed milk are fillings. The starch itself is cassava, a crop with deep Indigenous roots in Brazil. Use commercial tapioca starch.

Cultural background

A good tapioca has a lacy edge and a flexible center. Queijo coalho is a traditional salty cheese. This filling uses a firm cheese and notes coconut as the vegan option.

Why it tastes this way

Tapioca starch has almost no flavor and a lot of chew, so the filling is the taste: salty cheese or sweet coconut. The pan hydrates the starch into a sheet, which is a different chemistry from wheat gluten. Cassava is the Indigenous and later national starch. The crepe format is a modern street breakfast. Both can be true.

How it is made

Moistened tapioca starch is cooked as a crepe and filled.

Authentic touch

Moisten the starch until it holds when squeezed and still crumbles. Scatter it in an even layer in a moderate pan. Do not stir. It will knit itself. Flip once, quickly.

How it is used

Folded, hot. Cheese is the dairy filling. Coconut and a little sugar are the vegan one.

Field notes

  • The crepe is tapioca starch, not wheat.
  • Cheese is one filling. Coconut and sugar are the vegan one.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups tapioca starch
  • About 3/4 cup water
  • 1 cup grated firm cheese, or 1 cup grated coconut plus 2 tablespoons sugar

Steps

  1. Sprinkle water over the starch and toss until it clumps when squeezed and still crumbles.
  2. Scatter a thin even layer in a medium pan. Do not stir.
  3. When the sheet knits, flip it.
  4. Add cheese, or coconut and sugar, and fold.
  5. Serve immediately.
  6. Use commercial tapioca starch.

Use commercial cassava flour or yuca sold ready to cook. Do not process bitter cassava at home from these pages.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Coconut and sugar instead of cheese keep it vegan.
  • Beiju is the plainer cassava bread.

Pairings

  • cheese
  • coconut
