Pronounced RAG-moonk

Grated raw potato in a batter, fried, with pork and lingonberry.

20 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 4 · about 8 cakes

History

Raggmunk is a Swedish potato pancake made with raw grated potato in a batter, as opposed to rårakor, which are grated potato fried without much batter. Both are served with salted pork and lingonberries. The combination is a classic Swedish everyday meal.

Cultural background

The cake is crisp at the edge. The berries and the pork do the sweet-and-salt work. This page includes the pork as the traditional plate and marks the meat allergen.

Why it tastes this way

Raw potato fries up sweeter and lacier than mashed potato. The batter of flour, egg, and milk holds the shreds into a cake you can flip. Lingonberry cuts the fry. Salt pork is the preserved meat of a winter kitchen. Together they are a balanced plate from a cold climate that stored potatoes, berries, and cured pork.

How it is made

Raw potato is stirred into a thin batter and fried as cakes, served with pork and lingonberry.

Authentic touch

Squeeze some water from the potato, not all of the starch. A wet batter steams. Fry in a generous film of fat until the cake is actually crisp.

How it is used

With fried salt pork or bacon and lingonberry jam.

Field notes

  • Raggmunk has batter. Rårakor are mostly potato.
  • Lingonberry and pork are the documented partners.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and grated
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Oil or butter for frying
  • 8 ounces bacon or salt pork
  • Lingonberry jam

Steps

  1. Squeeze the grated potato lightly and mix with flour, egg, milk, and salt.
  2. Fry the pork until browned and set it aside.
  3. Fry spoonfuls of batter in the fat until crisp on both sides.
  4. Drain.
  5. Serve with the pork and jam.
  6. Keep the heat moderate so the potato cooks through.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Rårakor, on the next page, skip most of the batter.
  • A meatless plate is possible and is not the classic raggmunk meal.

Pairings

  • bacon
  • lingonberry jam
