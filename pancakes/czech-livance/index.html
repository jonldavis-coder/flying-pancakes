Czech lívance

You come in by Train. Czechia · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced LEE-vahn-tseh

Small yeasted Czech pancakes served with quark and jam, not rolled.

1 hr 20 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 16 small pancakes

  • No chili heat

History

Lívance are small yeast pancakes from Czechia. They are thicker than palačinky and are served in a pile with tvaroh and jam on top, not rolled around a filling. The yeast batter needs time to rise. They are a home dessert and a substantial sweet breakfast. This page keeps them separate from the thin crepe.

Cultural background

A special small pan is sometimes used. A ordinary frying pan works if you keep the cakes small and thick. Jam and fresh cheese are spooned on after cooking.

Why it tastes this way

Yeast gives them a mild fermented flavor and an open crumb. They brown in a little fat and taste toasted at the rim. Cool quark and tart jam on a hot cake is the contrast. They are less creamy inside than a filled crepe because the cheese stays on top.

How it is made

A yeast batter of flour, milk, and egg rises, then is cooked as small thick cakes and topped with quark and jam.

Authentic touch

Do not roll them. Spoon quark and jam onto the hot cakes. Let the batter rise until it is bubbly before you fry.

How it is used

In a pile, with quark and jam, as dessert or a sweet breakfast.

Field notes

  • Lívance are small and yeasted. Palačinky are thin crepes.
  • The cheese and jam go on top. The cakes are not rolled.
  • They need a rise before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain wheat flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 300 ml warm milk
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan
  • 200 g quark
  • Jam, for serving

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, egg, and warm milk into a thick batter. Cover until bubbly, about 1 hour.
  2. Stir it down. Cook small thick rounds in butter until both sides are brown and the center is done.
  3. Stack them on plates.
  4. Top with quark and jam.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Plum jam, a very typical fruit
  • Thin palačinky, the rolled crepe page

Pairings

  • Quark or farmer cheese
  • Jam
  • A little melted butter
