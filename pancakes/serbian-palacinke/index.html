Serbian palačinke

You come in by Train. Serbia · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced pah-lah-CHEEN-keh

Thin Serbian crepes rolled with plum jam.

15 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 crepes

History

Palačinke are Serbia's thin crepes, a standard home dessert. Plum jam is the filling written here, because plums are a Serbian pantry fruit. A chocolate-hazelnut spread is a modern habit in many homes. This recipe does not use it, and it does not name a brand. The Croatian page is a fresh-cheese roll. The two are neighbors, not copies. Ground walnuts are a real filling and would add a tree-nut allergen, so they stay a variation.

Cultural background

Palačinke are made for children and for guests, stacked and rolled. They are not a single saint's day. A wide pan and a thin batter are enough.

Why it tastes this way

Plum jam is dark, tart, and thick, so it stands up to a buttery crepe. The crepe itself is mild and spotted from the pan. Sugar in the batter is low. The jam does the sweetening. A thin roll means every bite has fruit and toasted surface.

How it is made

Thin wheat crepes are cooked and rolled around plum jam.

Authentic touch

Use plum jam, not a branded spread. If you fill them with walnuts, remove the nut-free diet. The Croatian cheese filling is a different page.

How it is used

As a snack or dessert, dusted with sugar if the jam is very tart.

Field notes

  • This page uses plum jam. It does not use a trademark spread.
  • Walnut fillings are traditional and are not nut-free.
  • The Croatian recipe in this journal is a cheese roll.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 350 ml whole milk
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan
  • 200 g plum jam

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, salt, eggs, and milk. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Cook thin crepes in a buttered pan.
  3. Spread plum jam on each crepe.
  4. Roll and serve.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Ground walnuts and sugar, a tree-nut filling
  • The Croatian fresh-cheese palačinke

Pairings

  • Plum jam
  • A glass of yogurt to drink
  • Powdered sugar
