Irregular fried dough pieces, crisp and puffy, with cheese or honey.

10 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · about 16 pieces

History

Petulla are Albanian fried dough pieces, a yeast or baking-powder batter dropped into oil. They are eaten with white cheese, honey, or powdered sugar, especially at breakfast or for guests. They are fritters rather than flipped pancakes, and they are a real national snack.

Cultural background

The shape is ragged on purpose. Cheese on the side is the savory way. Honey is the sweet way. This batter is the plain one, leavened with baking powder for a same-morning fry.

Why it tastes this way

Hot oil puffs a wet dough into something crisp outside and soft inside, a texture a dry griddle cannot make. White cheese is salty and is the contrast. Honey is the sweet contrast. Albania’s table often puts cheese and honey in the same breakfast orbit, even if not always on the same bite. The irregular edge is what holds either topping.

How it is made

A thick batter is fried in spoonfuls until puffed and golden.

Authentic touch

The oil should be hot enough that a drop of batter rises at once. Too cool, and petulla drink oil. Drop the batter from a spoon so the edges stay ragged.

How it is used

With white cheese or honey, not necessarily both.

Field notes

  • Petulla are fried batter pieces, not round diner pancakes.
  • Cheese and honey are both served. They are different plates.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup water
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Stir flour, baking powder, salt, and water into a thick batter.
  2. Heat 1 inch of oil over medium.
  3. Drop spoonfuls and fry until golden and cooked through, turning once.
  4. Drain.
  5. Serve with cheese or honey.
  6. The centers should not be doughy.

Fry over steady heat and do not leave the pot.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A yeast batter rested an hour is more traditional.
  • Flija is the layered baked pie, a different Albanian specialty.

Pairings

  • white cheese
  • honey
