Nalysnyky

You come in by Train. Ukraine · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced nah-LIS-nih-kih

Ukrainian crepes rolled with cheese and baked in sour cream.

30 min prep · 30 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 rolled crepes

History

Nalysnyky are Ukrainian filled crepes. A cheese filling baked under sour cream is the version written here. Meat fillings are also traditional and would make the dish not vegetarian. They are feast and Sunday food as well as a way to use cheese. They are not Russian blinchiki, which this journal fries after rolling, and they are not deruny, the potato pancakes.

Cultural background

The crepes are thin wheat sheets. The dish is finished in the oven so the sour cream thickens around the rolls. It can be a main course.

Why it tastes this way

Farmer cheese is mild and slightly sour. Egg and a pinch of salt keep the filling savory rather than dessert-sweet. This page does not sugar the cheese. Sour cream browns at the edges and sauces the crepes. Butter in the crepe batter tastes toasted. A sweet nalysnyk exists. This one is the savory cheese bake.

How it is made

Thin crepes are filled with seasoned farmer cheese, rolled, covered with sour cream, and baked until bubbling.

Authentic touch

Bake them in sour cream, covered for part of the time, so the crepes soften and the sauce thickens. Do not serve them as a dry roll if you want this dish.

How it is used

Hot, as a savory meal or a festive dish, with a salad. A sweet filling is a different plate.

Field notes

  • Nalysnyky are Ukrainian filled crepes.
  • This version is savory cheese baked in sour cream.
  • Deruny, the potato pancakes, are a different page.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain wheat flour
  • 2 large eggs, plus 1 for the filling
  • 350 ml whole milk
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt, divided
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan
  • 400 g farmer cheese, drained
  • 300 ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, 2 eggs, milk, and 1/4 tsp salt into a thin batter. Rest 15 minutes and cook eight crepes in butter.
  2. Mix the farmer cheese with the remaining egg, the rest of the salt, and the dill.
  3. Fill and roll the crepes. Lay them in a buttered baking dish and spoon the sour cream over them.
  4. Bake at 190°C until the cream is bubbling and the edges brown, about 20 minutes.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A meat filling, which adds a meat allergen
  • A sweet cheese and raisin filling, closer to a dessert

Pairings

  • The sour cream they bake in
  • Dill
  • A green salad
