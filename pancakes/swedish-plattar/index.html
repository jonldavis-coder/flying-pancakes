Pronounced PLET-ar

Tiny round pancakes from a special iron, with jam or berries.

10 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · about 30 small pancakes

History

Plättar are small Swedish pancakes cooked in a plättpanna, a pan with several round wells. They are the miniature form of the Swedish pancake and a cousin of the larger pannkakor. Lingonberry jam is the classic partner. The Upper Midwest church-supper pancake is the emigrant version of this batter.

Cultural background

They are a weeknight dessert or a weekend breakfast. Without the special pan, a spoon of batter in a small skillet makes one cake at a time and is less charming and still the recipe.

Why it tastes this way

A thin, eggy batter cooks into a tender coin because the well is small and hot. Lingonberries are the tart forest fruit preserved as jam, and their acid is why a rich little cake does not taste cloying. Butter in the wells browns the edges. The pan, not a different flour, is what separates plättar from a plate-sized pancake.

How it is made

A thin egg batter is cooked in a plättpanna or æbleskiver-style pan and served with jam.

Authentic touch

Butter each well, not just the first one. The cakes stick the moment the fat is gone. Flip them with a skewer when the tops look dry.

How it is used

Lingonberry jam, or butter and sugar.

Field notes

  • The plättpanna is the traditional tool.
  • Lingonberry jam is the usual topping. Cranberry is a cousin, not the same berry.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • Lingonberry jam, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk a thin batter and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Butter the wells of a plättpanna over medium heat.
  3. Fill each well with a thin layer.
  4. Flip when the tops set.
  5. Serve with lingonberry jam.
  6. Keep them warm in a low oven if you are cooking many.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Swedish pannkakor are the larger thin pancakes.
  • Swedish American pancakes are the same idea at a Midwestern supper.

Pairings

  • lingonberry jam
  • butter
  • sugar
