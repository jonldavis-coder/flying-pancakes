Arriving in Asturias

Frixuelos

You come in by Train. Spain · Southern Europe.

Pronounced free-SHWEH-los

Asturian Carnival pancakes, a little richer than Galician filloas, with lemon.

15 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 pancakes

History

Frixuelos are the Carnival pancakes of Asturias, thin egg crepes related to Galician filloas and not the same recipe. This batter uses lemon zest and a little extra egg, and it is finished with sugar or honey. Carnival is the season. They are not Entroido filloas, not Spanish tortitas, and not a tortilla de patatas. Honey, if you choose it at the table, is a honey allergen. The written ingredients stop at sugar.

Cultural background

Asturian Carnival has its own fried pastries. Frixuelos are the griddled ones, made at home and eaten as they come off the pan. They are a seasonal dessert that people also cook later in the year.

Why it tastes this way

More egg makes the sheet richer and more yellow than the anise filloa. Lemon zest brightens the butter. Sugar on top stays crystalline for a moment. They brown in spots and stay flexible. The flavor is lemon and egg, not anise.

How it is made

A thin batter with lemon zest is cooked as crepes and folded with sugar.

Authentic touch

Keep them distinct from filloas: lemon here, anise on the Galician page. Cook them thin. Sugar is the default finish. Honey is a choice, not a requirement.

How it is used

During Asturian Carnival, folded with sugar, as a sweet plate.

Field notes

  • Frixuelos are the Asturian Carnival pancake.
  • They are related to Galician filloas and they are not the same batter.
  • This page uses lemon. The filloa page uses anise.

Ingredients

  • 125 g plain wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar, plus more for serving
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 300 ml whole milk
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, salt, eggs, milk, and lemon zest into a thin batter. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Cook thin pancakes in butter until both sides are golden in spots.
  3. Sprinkle with sugar and fold.
  4. Serve warm.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A splash of apple brandy in the batter
  • Galician filloas, the anise Carnival pancake

Pairings

  • Sugar
  • Honey, which adds a honey allergen
  • A glass of milk
