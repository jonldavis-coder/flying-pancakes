Taiwanese danbing

You come in by Plane. Taiwan · East Asia.

Pronounced dahn-BING

A thin breakfast crepe with egg cooked on its face, brushed with soy sauce and rolled with scallion.

10 min prep · 12 min cook · Serves 2 · 2 rolled crepes

History

Danbing is Taiwan's egg crepe, a breakfast from street griddles and from the windows of morning shops. Dan means egg. A thin wheat batter is spread, an egg is cooked on it, soy sauce and scallion go over the top, and the sheet is rolled. Fillings such as pork floss or cheese are common additions. This recipe keeps the egg crepe itself, without meat, so the tags stay vegetarian. It is Taipei breakfast food, fast and savory.

Cultural background

The griddle is flat and lightly oiled. The crepe has to stay flexible enough to roll and strong enough to hold the egg. Breakfast stands assemble it in a minute. At home the same batter works in a nonstick pan. Pork floss, if you add it, brings a meat allergen the written recipe does not have.

Why it tastes this way

Egg against a toasted wheat sheet is the whole flavor, salted hard by soy sauce. Scallion cuts the richness. Because the egg is spread thin, it tastes fried rather than like a folded omelet. The crepe itself is almost bland, a carrier, which is why the sauce matters. It is a breakfast built for eating out of a bag.

How it is made

Cook a thin wheat crepe, spread an egg on it with soy sauce and scallion, and roll it tight.

Authentic touch

Spread the egg while the crepe is still moist so they fuse. Brush soy sauce on the egg, not only on the plate, or the roll tastes flat.

How it is used

Eat it immediately, rolled, as a street breakfast. A hot soy milk is the usual cup beside it.

Field notes

  • Danbing means egg pancake. It is a Taiwanese breakfast crepe.
  • The plain egg roll is vegetarian. Pork floss is an addition, not the base recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose wheat flour
  • 2/3 cup water
  • A pinch of salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • Neutral oil

Steps

  1. Whisk flour, water, and salt into a thin batter.
  2. Oil a pan over medium heat. Pour half the batter and swirl a thin crepe.
  3. When the top is nearly set, crack an egg onto it and spread the white and yolk into a thin layer.
  4. Brush with soy sauce and scatter scallion. Cook until the egg sets.
  5. Roll the crepe tightly. Repeat with the second egg.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Pork floss in the roll, which adds meat
  • A slice of processed cheese, which adds dairy

Pairings

  • Sweet or salty soy milk
  • A few slices of cucumber
  • Chile crisp on the side
