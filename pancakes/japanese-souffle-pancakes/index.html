Japanese Soufflé Pancakes

Very tall, jiggly pancakes of whipped egg white, a modern café style.

20 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 2 · 4 tall pancakes

History

Soufflé pancakes became a Japanese café fashion in the 2010s: egg whites whipped and folded into a small amount of batter, cooked slowly in rings so the cakes stand high and shake. They are a modern style, not a Meiji-era household recipe, and they should be dated that way. They are a dessert breakfast.

Cultural background

They need time and a low heat. A cover traps steam. They deflate as they cool, which is expected. Powdered sugar and butter are the finish.

Why it tastes this way

Whipped whites are almost the entire structure, so the cake tastes of egg and steam rather than of a dense crumb. The café style chased height. That is a recent commercial aesthetic, and the science is still a soufflé cooked in a pan. Low heat keeps the outside from browning before the inside sets.

How it is made

Whipped egg whites are folded into a yolk batter and cooked covered, slowly, in tall rounds.

Authentic touch

Do not open the lid for the first several minutes. Rings or a deep spoon shape help them climb. If the heat is high they brown and then collapse into a sad omelette.

How it is used

Immediately, with butter and powdered sugar or fruit.

Field notes

  • This is a 2010s café style, not an old household pancake.
  • They are supposed to jiggle and then slowly deflate.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • Oil
  • Powdered sugar

Steps

  1. Whisk yolks, sugar, milk, flour, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Whip the whites to firm peaks and fold them in.
  3. Oil two rings on a medium-low griddle. Fill them with batter.
  4. Cover and cook about 6 minutes, until the tops look set.
  5. Flip carefully and cook covered until the centers are just done.
  6. Dust with powdered sugar and serve immediately.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A little cocoa makes a chocolate soufflé pancake and is still modern.
  • Ordinary hotcakes are the diner style on the next page.

Pairings

  • powdered sugar
  • butter
  • berries
