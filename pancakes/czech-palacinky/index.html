Czech palačinky

You come in by Train. Czechia · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced PAH-lah-chin-kee

Thin Czech crepes filled with sweet quark and raisins.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 filled crepes

History

Palačinky are the thin crepes of Czechia, part of the same Central European family as Slovak palačinky and Hungarian palacsinta. This page is the Czech home version filled with tvaroh, the local quark, and raisins. Jam and cocoa are equally common and are variations. Lívance, the small yeast pancakes, are a different page. These are rolled crepes, not small risen cakes.

Cultural background

They are a dessert made in home kitchens and sold in cafes. The batter often includes a splash of sparkling water for a lighter sheet. That is included here.

Why it tastes this way

The crepe is thin and slightly sweet. Tvaroh is tangy, closer to the Austrian topfen filling than to a jam roll, which is why this page chose it. Raisins add chew. A light browning in butter keeps the outside interesting. They should bend, not crack.

How it is made

Thin crepes are cooked and rolled around a mixture of quark, sugar, egg yolk, and raisins.

Authentic touch

Use quark or a dry farmer cheese for tvaroh. The Slovak page in this journal uses a cocoa filling so the two recipes are not copies. Jam is still a true Czech serving.

How it is used

Warm or at room temperature, as a snack or dessert, dusted with sugar.

Field notes

  • Palačinky are thin crepes. Lívance are the small yeast pancakes.
  • Tvaroh is the Czech quark used in this filling.
  • The Slovak page uses a different filling so the two are not the same recipe.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar, plus 2 tbsp for the filling
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 2 large eggs, plus 1 yolk
  • 250 ml whole milk
  • 100 ml sparkling water
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan
  • 300 g quark or farmer cheese, drained
  • 40 g raisins

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, 1 tbsp sugar, salt, 2 eggs, milk, and sparkling water. Rest 10 minutes and cook thin crepes in butter.
  2. Mix the quark, egg yolk, 2 tbsp sugar, and raisins.
  3. Fill and roll the crepes.
  4. Serve dusted with sugar, warm or cool.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Jam only
  • Slovak palačinky with a cocoa filling, on the Slovak page

Pairings

  • Powdered sugar
  • A spoon of fruit jam beside the cheese
  • Cocoa and sugar, as the variation
