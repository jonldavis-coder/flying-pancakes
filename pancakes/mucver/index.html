Pronounced MEWJ-ver

Zucchini fritters with dill and a little cheese, fried as small cakes.

20 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · about 12 fritters

History

Mücver are Turkish zucchini fritters, grated squash bound with egg, flour, dill, and often a little white cheese, fried in spoonfuls. They are a meze and a home side. They are fritters, included as the vegetable cake of the Turkish table.

Cultural background

They should be crisp outside and soft inside. Yogurt with a little garlic is a common sauce and adds more dairy.

Why it tastes this way

Zucchini carries water, so squeezing it is what lets the fritter fry instead of steam. Dill is the herb that makes them taste Turkish rather than like a generic latke. A little cheese salts the mild squash. The fry is shallow. Yogurt on the side is cool and tangy.

How it is made

Grated, squeezed zucchini is mixed with egg, flour, dill, and cheese and fried as small cakes.

Authentic touch

Salt and squeeze the zucchini dry. Fry in enough oil to brown the sides. Do not make the cakes large.

How it is used

Warm, with yogurt.

Field notes

  • Squeeze the zucchini or the fritters will be soggy.
  • Dill and white cheese are the usual seasonings.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium zucchini, grated
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup crumbled white cheese
  • 2 tablespoons chopped dill
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Salt the zucchini, rest 10 minutes, and squeeze dry.
  2. Mix with eggs, flour, cheese, and dill.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in a shallow layer of oil until golden.
  4. Drain.
  5. Serve with yogurt.
  6. Keep them small so the center cooks.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Herb mücver without squash is closer to kaygana and is a different balance.
  • A spoon of cornmeal lightens the batter.

Pairings

  • yogurt
  • dill
