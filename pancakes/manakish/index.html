A flatbread topped with za'atar and olive oil, baked or cooked on a griddle.

History

Manakish, or manaqish, are Levantine flatbreads topped with za'atar and olive oil, a breakfast bread across Lebanon, Syria, Palestine, and Jordan. Bakeries bake them. A covered skillet is a home method and is the one written here, so the bread sits honestly among griddled foods. Za'atar is thyme, sesame, and sumac.

Cultural background

They are eaten warm, folded, with tea, labneh, or vegetables. Sesame in the za'atar is an allergen. This recipe assumes a za'atar blend that contains sesame and says so.

Why it tastes this way

Olive oil carries the thyme and sumac so the bread tastes herbal, sour, and rich rather than plain. Sumac’s lemony acidity is the Levantine note. The dough is a simple yeast dough, softer than a cracker. Breakfast bakeries made manakish a daily bread. The skillet version is supper or breakfast at home when the bakery is shut.

How it is made

Small yeasted rounds are topped with za'atar and oil and cooked in a covered skillet.

Authentic touch

Dimple the dough, spread oil and za'atar to the edge, and cook covered so the top sets while the bottom browns. Do not drown it in so much oil that it fries into a doughnut.

How it is used

Warm, folded.

Field notes

  • Za'atar and oil are the classic topping.
  • Many za'atar blends contain sesame.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 3 tablespoons za'atar
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, salt, water, and 1 tablespoon oil. Rise 1 hour.
  2. Divide into 4 and flatten.
  3. Mix za'atar and olive oil and spread it on the dough.
  4. Cook in a covered skillet over medium until the bottom is spotted and the top looks set.
  5. Serve warm.
  6. Fold, do not stack uncovered.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Cheese manakish add akkawi or similar and are then dairy.
  • A bakery oven gives a drier crust. The flavor is the same topping.

Pairings
