Sweet corn kernels in a soft pancake.

15 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · about 8 pancakes

History

Hotcakes de elote fold fresh or canned sweet corn into the Mexican breakfast pancake. They are a home and fonda variation, using the country’s central grain in a modern batter. They are not corn tortillas and not gorditas. The wheat batter is still there, so they are not gluten-free.

Cultural background

The corn should be sweet. A savory chile pancake would be a different dish. Butter and honey or cajeta finish them.

Why it tastes this way

Sweet corn adds moisture and a summer flavor to a mild batter. Mexico’s long maize agriculture is why corn is the grain people reach for, even inside a wheat café pancake. The combination is modern. The corn is old. Stating both is the accurate way to write the page.

How it is made

Sweet corn is folded into a hotcake batter and cooked on a griddle.

Authentic touch

Pat the kernels dry. Wet corn makes the center soggy. Fold them in. Do not blend them if you want the bites.

How it is used

With butter and honey.

Field notes

  • The corn is the local grain inside a modern wheat pancake.
  • Pat the kernels dry so the center sets.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 cup corn kernels, patted dry

Steps

  1. Mix the batter and fold in the corn.
  2. Cook on a buttered medium griddle.
  3. Flip when the edges set.
  4. Serve with honey.
  5. Pat the corn dry first.
  6. The centers should be cooked.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Blended corn makes a more custardy batter and a different texture.
  • Plain hotcakes are the page without corn.

Pairings

  • honey
  • butter
