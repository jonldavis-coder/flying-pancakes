Pronounced BLEE-nee

Yeasted wheat pancakes, lightly sour, full of small holes.

1 hr 40 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 6 · about 16 blini

History

Blini are yeasted Russian pancakes. During Maslenitsa, the week before the Orthodox Great Lent, they are the festival food, tied in folk explanation to the sun and to using up butter and eggs before the fast. Maslenitsa is a real holiday on the Orthodox calendar. The sun symbolism is folk interpretation, so it is named here as that, not as doctrine. Blini are also eaten the rest of the year with butter, sour cream, herring, or caviar.

Cultural background

A risen batter makes them different from a baking-powder pancake. Toppings are rich because the week before Lent was for finishing dairy. Caviar is a famous topping and is not required.

Why it tastes this way

Yeast and a warm rest give a slight tang and a holey crumb that holds melted butter. Maslenitsa explains the butter: the fast ahead limited dairy, so the week used it. Wheat blini are milder than buckwheat ones. Sour cream and salt fish are the savory logic of a rich, mild cake. The holiday is religious and seasonal, which is why the same batter tastes like a feast in that week and like breakfast later.

How it is made

A yeasted batter of flour, milk, egg, and butter is risen twice and cooked as small pancakes.

Authentic touch

Let the batter rise until puffy. Cook the blini smaller than a dinner plate so they stay tender. Brush the cooked side with butter if you want the classic sheen.

How it is used

Butter, sour cream, smoked fish, or jam. Herring and caviar are traditional and add fish.

Field notes

  • Maslenitsa is the Orthodox pre-Lenten week, and blini are its pancake.
  • The sun story is folk symbolism, not a verified ancient rite this page can date.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups warm milk
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the pan

Steps

  1. Stir milk, yeast, sugar, and flour. Rest 45 minutes until bubbly.
  2. Beat in salt, egg, and melted butter. Rest 30 minutes more.
  3. Cook small pancakes on a buttered medium griddle.
  4. Flip when the surface is full of holes.
  5. Brush with butter.
  6. Serve warm with sour cream.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Buckwheat blini are the earthier Maslenitsa cousin.
  • A spoon of batter saved as a starter makes the next batch sourer.

Pairings

  • butter
  • sour cream
  • jam
