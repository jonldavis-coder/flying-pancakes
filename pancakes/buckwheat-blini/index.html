Buckwheat blini

You come in by Train. Russia · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced BUCK-wheat BLEE-nee

Yeasted buckwheat blini, earthier than the wheat cakes, for butter and sour cream.

1 hr 40 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 4 · 12 blini

History

Buckwheat blini, grechnevye bliny, are the other classic Russian blin. Buckwheat and wheat blini both belong to Maslenitsa, butter week, which is not one Tuesday. This page is buckwheat only, so it is gluten-free if the flour was milled apart from wheat. The wheat page is separate. The batter is still yeasted. Sour cream, butter, and, on a festive table, herring or caviar are the toppings. Herring is not in this recipe.

Cultural background

Buckwheat was a peasant staple and a feast grain. The cakes are smaller and more assertive than white blini. They are still butter-week food: dairy, not a lean fast.

Why it tastes this way

Buckwheat tastes earthy and slightly bitter. Yeast adds a sour edge that fits sour cream. Butter softens the bitterness without hiding it. They brown faster than wheat cakes and stay a little drier. Salt in the batter matters more because the grain is strong.

How it is made

Buckwheat flour, yeast, milk, egg, and butter rise into a batter and are cooked as small thick blini.

Authentic touch

Do not sweeten them like dessert crepes. Sour cream and butter are the pairing. If you need them gluten-free, buy buckwheat that was not milled with wheat. A mixed wheat-and-buckwheat batter is honest and is no longer this page.

How it is used

With butter and sour cream during butter week or at any rich breakfast. Smoked fish is an optional topping and adds a fish allergen.

Field notes

  • Buckwheat blini and wheat blini are both traditional. This page is buckwheat.
  • Maslenitsa is a week, not a Tuesday.
  • There is no wheat in this batter. Cross-contact depends on how the flour was milled.

Ingredients

  • 200 g buckwheat flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 350 ml warm milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 30 g unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  • Sour cream, for serving

Steps

  1. Whisk the buckwheat flour, yeast, sugar, milk, egg, salt, and melted butter. Cover until bubbly, about 1 hour. The batter will look darker than a wheat batter.
  2. Stir it down and loosen with a spoon of milk if it is stiff.
  3. Cook small rounds in butter until the tops are set, then turn and brown them.
  4. Serve hot with sour cream and more butter.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Half wheat flour, which removes the gluten-free diet and becomes a mixed blin
  • A spoon of sour cream stirred into the batter

Pairings

  • Sour cream
  • Butter
  • Pickled herring, for a fish plate
