Saffron Pancake of Gotland

A baked rice pancake perfumed with saffron, served with jam and cream.

30 min prep · 40 min cook · Serves 8 · one 8-inch dish

History

Gotland’s saffron pancake is a baked dessert of rice porridge, cream, eggs, and saffron, associated with the island and often with the Christmas season. Saffron also scents Swedish Lucia buns, so the spice is a Swedish holiday flavor, not a random addition. The pancake is cut in squares. It is not a stack.

Cultural background

Rice porridge is cooked first, then mixed with the rest and baked. Dewberry or blackberry jam and cream are traditional partners. If you cannot get dewberry, say you are using blackberry or another dark jam.

Why it tastes this way

Saffron’s aroma is floral and bitter in a pinch too large, which is why a small amount perfumes a rich rice custard without turning it medicinal. Rice pudding as a base makes the cake thick and soothing. Gotland kept this as an island dessert. Holiday saffron links it to Lucia season, when the same spice is in the buns.

How it is made

Rice is cooked to a porridge, mixed with cream, egg, sugar, and saffron, and baked.

Authentic touch

Bloom the saffron in a spoon of warm milk so the color and smell spread. Stirring threads straight into a cold batter leaves yellow streaks and blank bites.

How it is used

Warm or cool, with dark jam and whipped cream.

Field notes

  • This is a Gotland baked pudding-pancake, not a crêpe.
  • Saffron is the defining spice, used in a small amount.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup short-grain rice
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • Butter for the dish

Steps

  1. Simmer the rice in the milk until soft and thick, about 20 minutes. Cool slightly.
  2. Bloom the saffron in a spoon of warm milk and stir it in.
  3. Beat in eggs, sugar, cream, and flour.
  4. Pour into a buttered dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F about 35 minutes until set and browned.
  6. Serve with jam and cream.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A pinch of cardamom beside the saffron is a home choice. Saffron should remain the main perfume.
  • Skip the saffron and you have a plain rice pancake, not the Gotland dish.

Pairings

  • blackberry jam
  • whipped cream
