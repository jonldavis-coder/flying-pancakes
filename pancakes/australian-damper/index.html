History

Damper is a simple Australian colonial bread of flour, water, and a raising agent, traditionally cooked in the coals of a campfire. Stockmen made it because it needed no yeast. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have their own breads. Damper is the settler camp bread, and some Aboriginal communities also adopted related baking-powder breads. This page does not rename damper as an ancient Indigenous recipe. A covered heavy pot in an oven or on a stove is the home stand-in for coals.

Cultural background

It is a bread for butter, golden syrup, or a stew. The crust is thick. The crumb is dense.

Why it tastes this way

Flour, salt, and baking powder make a bread where there is no starter and no oven, which is why it tastes plain and a little dry unless it is fresh and buttered. The campfire crust is the romance. The chemistry is baking powder. Stating the colonial origin avoids a false Indigenous attribution while leaving room for the later shared use of similar breads.

How it is made

Flour, baking powder, salt, and water are patted into a round and baked in a covered pot.

Authentic touch

Do not knead it like yeast bread. Pat it, bake it until the crust is firm and the center is cooked, and eat it the day it is made. Coals are not required for this version.

How it is used

Warm, with butter or golden syrup.

Field notes

  • Damper is settler camp bread, not an ancient Indigenous recipe.
  • Baking powder is the leaven. No yeast is required.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 3/4 cup water

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Mix a soft dough and pat it into a round.
  3. Bake in a covered pot about 35 minutes, until the crust is firm and the center is cooked.
  4. Cool 10 minutes.
  5. Serve with butter.
  6. Eat it the day it is made.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A handful of raisins makes a sweeter damper.
  • Bannock is the related camp bread of Canada.

Pairings

  • butter
  • golden syrup
