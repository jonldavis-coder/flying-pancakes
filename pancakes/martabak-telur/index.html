A thin crisp wrapper around a spiced egg-and-scallion filling.

History

Martabak telur is the savory martabak: a thin stretched dough wrapped around beaten egg, scallion, and spices, fried until crisp. It is night-market food across Indonesia and Malaysia. Meat is sometimes in the egg. This filling is egg and scallion, so it has no meat. The sweet martabak is a different batter.

Cultural background

The wrapper should be thin and blistered. The egg sets inside. It is cut into squares and eaten with a pickle or a vinegar sauce.

Why it tastes this way

A thin oiled dough fries crisp, and the egg inside steams, so you get crackle and custard in one square. Scallion and pepper are the savor. Street cooks stretch the dough until it is nearly transparent. A home cook can roll it thin and still tell the truth. The curry-leaf or leek note in some regions is local. Scallion is the widely used green.

How it is made

Thin dough packets filled with seasoned egg are pan-fried until crisp.

Authentic touch

Roll or stretch the dough thin, add a thin layer of egg, fold, and fry until the egg is cooked and the outside is blistered. A thick wrapper is doughy.

How it is used

Hot, in squares, with pickled cucumber or a sour dip.

Field notes

  • Martabak telur is the savory crisp packet, not the sweet thick pancake.
  • The egg inside should be fully set.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 scallions, sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper

Steps

  1. Mix a soft dough and rest 20 minutes.
  2. Beat the eggs with scallions, pepper, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Roll the dough very thin, spread a thin layer of egg, and fold into a packet.
  4. Fry in a little oil until blistered and the egg is cooked.
  5. Cut into squares.
  6. Serve hot.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A little cooked minced meat in the egg is a common addition.
  • Martabak manis is the sweet thick pancake.

Pairings

  • pickled cucumber
