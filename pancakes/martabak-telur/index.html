Martabak telur

You come in by Plane. Indonesia · Southeast Asia.

Pronounced MAR-tah-bahk tuh-LOOR

A thin, crisp wheat skin folded around a juicy filling of egg, seasoned beef, and onion.

30 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 2 large squares

History

Martabak telur is the savory stuffed martabak, a thin wheat dough stretched wide, filled with egg and meat, folded, and fried. Indonesia sells it from night stalls. Malaysia eats the same family of stuffed bread, so Malaysia is listed too. A related bread in the Arabian Peninsula is called mutabbaq. This page does not add Saudi Arabia, and it does not treat the names as one recipe. The written filling is egg and beef, so the tags include egg and meat. It is not the sweet martabak manis.

Cultural background

The skill is the stretch. The dough should be nearly transparent before the filling goes on. Then it is folded into a square and fried until the skin blisters. Scallion, onion, and a little chile season the meat. The heat of this version is mild.

Why it tastes this way

The crust is fried laminated dough, crisp and oily in a thin layer. Egg sets around the beef and keeps the center moist. Onion and scallion sweeten the meat. A little soy sauce and white pepper make the filling savory without a heavy curry. You taste crunch first and a peppery meat custard second. The sweet martabak has none of that pepper.

How it is made

Stretch an oiled wheat dough, cover it with beaten egg and seasoned beef, fold it into a square, and fry it.

Authentic touch

Stretch the dough until it wants to tear, then patch any hole before you add the egg. A thick skin never becomes the blistered crust stalls are known for. Cook the square until the meat is done, not only until the outside browns.

How it is used

Cut the square into pieces and eat it hot, with a pickle or a cucumber relish. It is late-night street food.

Field notes

  • Martabak telur is the savory stuffed pancake of egg and meat, not the sweet thick martabak.
  • Malaysia shares the stuffed bread. Mutabbaq is a related name elsewhere.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose wheat flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 3 tablespoons neutral oil, plus more for frying
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 grams ground beef
  • 1 small onion, minced
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 green chile, minced

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, water, and 1 tablespoon of oil into a soft dough. Knead, then rest 30 minutes coated in oil.
  2. Mix beef, onion, scallion, soy sauce, and chile. Beat the eggs in a separate bowl.
  3. Divide the dough in half. Stretch one piece on an oiled board until it is very thin.
  4. Scatter half the beef over it and pour half the eggs on top. Fold the edges in to make a square.
  5. Fry in shallow oil over medium heat until both sides are brown and the beef is cooked through. Repeat.

Fry until the beef in the center is no longer pink.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Chicken instead of beef, still meat
  • Extra green chile for a hotter filling

Pairings

  • Cucumber and vinegar relish
  • A little chile sauce
  • Sweet tea
