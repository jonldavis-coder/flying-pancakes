Arriving in Iceland

Icelandic lummur

You come in by Boat. Iceland · Northern Europe.

Pronounced LUM-mur

Small thick Icelandic pancakes with rye, buttered and sugared.

10 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · 12 small pancakes

  • No chili heat

History

Lummur are small thick Icelandic pancakes, often made with some rye flour and served with butter and sugar. They are a different cake from the paper-thin pönnukökur. Rye and wheat together mean this recipe is not gluten-free. They are a home snack and breakfast, cooked in spoonfuls on a griddle.

Cultural background

Rye has a long place in Icelandic baking because wheat was not always the easy grain. A lumma is hearty for its size. Brown sugar or white sugar on butter is the serving.

Why it tastes this way

Rye tastes deeper and slightly sour next to white flour. Baking powder lifts the crumb so it is not a cracker. Butter on the hot cake melts into the holes. Sugar is the contrast. They should be thick enough that the center stays soft.

How it is made

Wheat flour, rye flour, baking powder, egg, and milk make a thick batter that is cooked in small rounds and buttered.

Authentic touch

Keep rye as part of the flour, not the whole of it, for this page. Cook them as small thick rounds. Butter and sugar are the topping, not a rolled jam filling.

How it is used

Warm, with butter and sugar, as a snack or breakfast.

Field notes

  • Lummur are small and thick. Pönnukökur are thin.
  • Rye in the batter is part of this version.
  • They are buttered and sugared, not usually rolled around a filling.

Ingredients

  • 100 g plain wheat flour
  • 50 g rye flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 250 ml whole milk
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan and serving

Steps

  1. Whisk both flours, the baking powder, sugar, and salt. Beat in the egg and milk until you have a thick batter.
  2. Cook small rounds in butter over medium heat until bubbles set, then turn.
  3. The center should spring back and not taste of raw rye.
  4. Butter them and sprinkle with sugar.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • All wheat flour, which loses the rye note and is still not gluten-free
  • Pönnukökur, the thin rolled pancakes

Pairings

  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • A spoon of jam if sugar is not enough
