Pronounced kluh-TSEE-teh

Thin pancakes with jam, chocolate, or sweet cheese.

15 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 crepes

History

Clătite are Romanian crepes, filled with jam, grated chocolate and walnuts, or sweet cheese. They are a common dessert. The walnut filling is traditional and is a tree nut, so this written recipe uses jam and notes the walnut version beside it.

Cultural background

They are rolled. A dust of sugar finishes them. Cheese clătite are closer to a blintz.

Why it tastes this way

The crepe is mild and the filling is sweet, a pattern across this part of Europe. Jam keeps the cake nut-free. Walnuts, when used, taste of the oil in the nut and of sugar, a filling that shows up because walnuts grow in the region. The batter is the shared thin egg batter.

How it is made

Thin crepes are rolled with jam.

Authentic touch

A nonstick or well-seasoned pan and a ladle of batter are enough. Do not add baking powder. Clătite are not fluffy pancakes.

How it is used

Warm, with sugar if the jam is tart.

Field notes

  • Clătite are crepes, not a thick pancake.
  • The jam filling keeps this version nut-free. Walnuts are the variation.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Jam, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk and rest the batter 10 minutes.
  2. Cook thin crepes.
  3. Fill with jam and roll.
  4. Serve warm.
  5. Sugar on top is optional.
  6. Keep the pan lightly oiled.

This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Ground walnuts with sugar are a classic filling and add tree nuts.
  • Sweet cheese is the other filling.

Pairings

  • jam
  • sugar
