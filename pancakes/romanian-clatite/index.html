Arriving in Romania

Romanian clătite

You come in by Train. Romania · Eastern Europe.

Pronounced kluh-TEE-teh

Thin Romanian crepes with jam or sweet cheese, a home dessert.

15 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · 8 crepes

History

Clătite are Romania's thin pancakes, rolled with jam, chocolate, or sweet cheese. This recipe uses jam and a little lemon in the batter, and it does not use a trademark spread. Sweet cheese is a variation. They are home dessert and a snack, in the same broad crepe family as other Central and Eastern European palačinke, with a Romanian name and a Romanian table. They are not a potato pancake.

Cultural background

A stack of clătite is weekend cooking. The batter is flour, egg, and milk, sometimes with sparkling water or a spoon of rum. This page uses lemon zest instead of alcohol so the cake stays simple.

Why it tastes this way

Lemon in the batter keeps the crepe from tasting only of flour. Jam provides the fruit. Butter browns small spots that taste toasted. They are thin, so the filling and the surface matter more than a fluffy crumb. A thick batter would make them heavy.

How it is made

A thin lemon batter is cooked as crepes and rolled with jam.

Authentic touch

Roll them around jam while warm. A cheese and raisin filling is the other home version. Skip branded chocolate spreads if you want the pantry recipe.

How it is used

As dessert or a snack, rolled, sometimes with a dusting of sugar.

Field notes

  • Clătite are thin Romanian crepes.
  • Jam and sweet cheese are both home fillings.
  • This batter has lemon zest and no trademark spread.

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 350 ml whole milk
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 30 g unsalted butter, for the pan
  • 200 g jam, for filling

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, salt, eggs, milk, and lemon zest into a thin batter. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Cook thin crepes in a buttered pan until spotted on both sides.
  3. Spread each with jam.
  4. Roll and serve warm.

Refrigerate leftovers and reheat on a dry skillet. Egg or dairy batter should not sit at room temperature. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Farmer cheese, sugar, and raisins
  • A spoon of cocoa and sugar, without a branded spread

Pairings

  • Sour cherry or apricot jam
  • Sweet farmer cheese, as a variation
  • Sugar
